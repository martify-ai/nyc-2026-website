Why this matters

This is for the young person who wants their life to actually count.

Every man God ever used for something great on earth had one thing in common — they walked with Him. Not just believed in Him. Walked with Him. That's the life this convention is calling you into: not religion, not rules, but a real, daily walk with God that shapes how you think, choose, and live.

If you're between 16 and 29 (or beyond) and you sense there's more to your life than what you're currently living — this week is for you.

What you'll walk away with

Five days built around one goal: helping you leave different from how you came.

A real encounter

Dr. Bimbo Lawal, Pastor Kayode Ibrahim, and other anointed ministers will be ministering across the week — not just speaking, but leading you into an actual encounter with God.

Direction for your next step

You'll get to sit with professionals from different fields who'll speak practically into your career, calling, and the decisions in front of you right now.

A community, not a crowd

Over 200 young people registered for the last edition. You won't be figuring this out alone — you'll be walking it out alongside others your age.
